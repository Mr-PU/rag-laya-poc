Acme Robotics — Leave & Time Off Policy

All full-time employees at Acme Robotics accrue 22 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at the start of January. Leave accrues at a rate of 1.83 days per month for employees who join partway through the year.

Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the following year. Any leave beyond that cap is forfeited unless local law requires otherwise. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 of the new year.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required only for sick leave lasting more than 3 consecutive working days.

Parental leave: primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave, and secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption date.

To request leave, submit a request through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance for planned leave. Emergency leave should be reported to your manager as soon as possible and logged retroactively.

Unused annual leave is paid out upon termination of employment, calculated at the employee's most recent daily base salary rate.
