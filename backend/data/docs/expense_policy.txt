Acme Robotics — Travel & Expense Policy

Employees traveling for business may book economy class flights for journeys under 6 hours and premium economy for journeys over 6 hours. Business class requires VP-level approval in advance.

Daily meal per-diem while traveling is capped at $75 in the United States and Europe, and $50 in all other regions. Receipts are required for any single expense over $25.

Hotel bookings should not exceed $250 per night in major cities (New York, London, Tokyo, San Francisco) and $150 per night elsewhere. Employees should use the corporate travel portal whenever possible.

All expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed except in extraordinary circumstances approved by Finance.

Reimbursement is processed within 10 business days of an approved expense report. Corporate card expenses are reconciled automatically; personal card expenses require manual reimbursement via the expense portal.

Client entertainment expenses over $200 require prior written approval from a department head.
