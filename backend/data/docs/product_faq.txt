Acme Robotics — RoboArm X1 Product FAQ

The RoboArm X1 has a maximum payload capacity of 5 kilograms and a reach of 850 millimeters. It operates on a standard 220V power supply and draws approximately 400 watts under typical load.

The unit ships with a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering parts and labor for manufacturing defects. Damage from misuse, unauthorized modification, or operation outside the rated payload is not covered.

Firmware updates are released quarterly and can be applied via the RoboArm Companion app over USB-C or Wi-Fi. Acme recommends updating firmware before any new deployment.

The RoboArm X1 supports ROS2 (Robot Operating System 2) out of the box, along with a Python SDK for custom automation scripts. Sample scripts are available in the `roboarm-examples` public repository.

Recommended operating temperature range is 5°C to 40°C, with humidity below 80% non-condensing. Operating outside this range may trigger the built-in thermal safety cutoff.

Customer support for the RoboArm X1 is available via the support portal, with a guaranteed first response time of 4 business hours for Priority customers and 1 business day for Standard customers.
